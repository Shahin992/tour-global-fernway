Refund & cancellation policy

Plans change. Here is exactly what happens to your money if you need to change or cancel, and what we do if we have to.

1. Changing your trip

You can move to a different date or trip free of charge up to 60 days before departure, once per booking. Your deposit and any payments move with you. Changes requested within 60 days of departure are treated as a cancellation.

2. If you cancel

Please tell us in writing. Charges are a percentage of the total trip price and depend on when we receive your notice:

Notice before departureCancellation charge
More than 60 daysYour deposit ($500 per person)
59 to 30 days50% of the trip price
29 to 15 days75% of the trip price
14 days or less100% of the trip price

Most travel insurance policies cover these charges if you cancel for reasons such as illness. Check your policy when you book.

3. Non-refundable items

Some costs are paid to third parties as soon as you book and cannot be recovered, whatever the notice period. These are listed on your booking confirmation and include:

  • Inca Trail and other government permits issued in your name.
  • Internal flights and train tickets booked on your request.
  • Safari park fees paid in advance to secure camps.

4. If we cancel or make a major change

If we cancel your trip, or make a significant change you do not accept, you can choose a full refund of everything you have paid us, or move to another trip with a $200 per person credit towards it. We are not responsible for flights or other arrangements you have booked separately, which is why we recommend waiting until a departure is Guaranteed before buying non-refundable flights.

5. Events beyond anyone’s control

If a trip cannot run safely because of war, natural disaster, epidemic, government action or similar events, we will offer a transfer to a later date or a refund of all money paid, less only non-refundable third-party costs we cannot recover.

6. Leaving a trip early

If you leave a trip after it has started, no refund is due for unused services, but we will do our best to recover costs from suppliers on your behalf. Your travel insurance may cover you if you leave for medical or family reasons.

7. How and when refunds are paid

We pay refunds to the original payment method within 14 days of cancellation. Because payments are held in a protected client account until your trip runs, your money is available to be returned.
