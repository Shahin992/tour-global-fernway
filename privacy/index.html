Privacy notice

We collect only what we need to plan and run your trip, we never sell it, and you can ask us to see or delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Fernway Journeys ("Fernway", "we", "us") plans and operates small-group and tailor-made trips. We are responsible for the personal data described in this notice. You can contact us about privacy at privacy@fernwayjourneys.com.

2. What we collect

  • Enquiry details: your name, email address, the trip you are interested in, travel dates and anything you tell us in your message.
  • Booking details: the names, dates of birth, nationalities and passport details of everyone travelling, plus contact and emergency-contact details.
  • Special category data: dietary requirements, medical conditions or mobility needs, but only when you choose to tell us so that we can look after you.
  • Payment details: processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Website data: basic server logs (IP address, browser and pages visited) kept for security for 30 days.

3. How we use it

We use your data to reply to enquiries, design and book your trip, buy permits and tickets in your name, keep you safe while travelling, meet our legal and accounting obligations, and, only if you ask us to, send you our monthly newsletter.

We rely on the performance of our contract with you, our legal obligations, your consent (for newsletters and for special category data) and our legitimate interest in running and improving our trips.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs to provide your trip:

  • Your leader and local partners, hotels, transport companies and permit authorities in the countries you visit.
  • Our payment provider and our bank.
  • Emergency services and medical providers, if needed to protect your vital interests.

We never sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. International transfers

Because our trips are international, your data is sent to suppliers in the countries you travel to, some of which have different data-protection laws from yours. We send only what is needed and require suppliers to keep it confidential and secure.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted after two years. Booking records are kept for seven years after travel for legal and accounting purposes. Medical and dietary information is deleted 90 days after your trip ends.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it or send it to you in a portable format, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA may give you further rights, including the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We reply to all requests within 30 days.

8. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It may store a small amount of information in your browser to remember interface choices; this stays on your device and is never sent to us.

9. Changes to this notice

We review this notice every year and whenever we change how we use data. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
