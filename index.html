What is Fernway?

Fernway Journeys is a small-group tour operator. We run guided trips for no more than twelve travellers, led by people who live where you are travelling, and we design private, tailor-made journeys in thirty-one countries. Our days are paced for walking, eating well and meeting people, not for ticking off sights from a coach window.

N° 03 · How we travel

Four rules we don’t bend.

01

Never more than twelve

Twelve is the most that fits round one table, in one minibus, in a mountain hut’s dining room. Our average group in 2026 was nine. That is small enough to stay in family-run inns and to be welcomed rather than processed, and big enough to make friends.

02

Led by people who live there

Every trip is led by someone from the region: a Kumano guide from Tanabe, a Quechua guide from Ollantaytambo, a mountain guide from Imlil. They choose the cafés, translate the jokes and know the path is closed before the sign goes up. Meet a few of them.

03

Slower days, fewer hotel changes

We build itineraries around two- and three-night stays with free afternoons written in. You will walk, ride or paddle most days, but you will not be packing your bag every morning, and lunch is a meal, not a sandwich on the bus.

04

A footprint we publish

Each itinerary shows its estimated footprint in kilograms of CO2e per traveller per day, excluding flights. We cut it where we can, with trains over internal flights, local food and refill stations instead of bottles, and we fund a conservation project in every region we visit. How we measure it.

N° 04 · Tailor-made

Your dates, your pace, our specialists.

Not every trip fits a group calendar. Our specialists have each lived in the regions they plan, and they build private journeys from the same guides, inns and trails our groups use.

  1. We listen

    A 30-minute video call with a specialist about where, when, how active and what matters most to you.

  2. We design your trip

    Within five working days you receive a day-by-day draft with a guide price, maps and the reasons behind each choice.

  3. We refine and book

    Change anything, as often as you like. A $500 deposit per person secures every hotel, guide and train.

  4. We are with you

    A local partner meets you on arrival, and a 24-hour support line connects you to someone who knows your plan.

Tailor-made trips usually start from about $350 per person per day, excluding international flights.

N° 06 · Questions

Before you ask.

The six questions we hear most. There are more on the full questions page, or ask a planner directly.

What is a small group tour, and how many people are in the group?

A small group tour is a guided trip with a fixed itinerary and dates, shared with a handful of other travellers and led by a professional leader. Fernway groups are capped at 12 travellers (8 on safari) and averaged 9 in 2026, so you stay in smaller inns and travel in one minibus rather than a coach.

How fit do I need to be?

Every trip has an activity level from 1 (Leisurely) to 5 (Demanding). Level 2 means up to four hours of easy walking a day; level 4 means six to eight hours on mountain paths with real climbs. Each trip page explains exactly what to expect, and our activity level guide helps you choose.

Are flights included in the price?

No. Prices cover everything from the first night’s accommodation to the last day’s departure: accommodation, your leader, transport, entry fees and the meals listed. International flights are not included because our travellers fly in from all over the world. We can suggest flight times that fit the itinerary.

Are small group tours good for solo travellers? Is there a single supplement?

Yes. Around 40% of our travellers come alone. We pair solo travellers with a roommate of the same gender at no extra cost; if you would prefer your own room, the single supplement is listed on each trip and is usually $450 to $900.

What happens if a departure doesn’t reach the minimum numbers?

Departures marked Guaranteed run regardless of numbers. Other dates need four travellers and are confirmed 60 days before departure. If a date does not run, you choose another date, another trip or a full refund of everything you have paid.

Is my money protected if something goes wrong?

Your payments are held in a protected client account and are only released to us after your trip has run. If we were ever unable to operate a trip you had paid for, that money would be returned to you.
