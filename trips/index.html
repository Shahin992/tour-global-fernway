Activity levels

What levels 1 to 5 actually mean.

The level describes the hardest consecutive days of a trip, not the average. If you’re between two levels, ask us; we’d rather talk it through than have you struggle on day three.

LevelA typical walking dayClimb per day2027 examples
1 · LeisurelyShort walks under 2 hours on even ground; most travel is by vehicle or boat.Under 150 mSerengeti & Ngorongoro
2 · EasyUp to 4 hours on good paths with gentle hills, usually with rest days between.Up to 400 mAtlas to Sahara, Iceland, Vietnam, Tuscany
3 · Moderate4 to 6 hours on varied paths and steps, several walking days in a row.Up to 800 mKumano Kodo, Petra & Wadi Rum, Lofoten
4 · Challenging6 to 8 hours on mountain trails, possibly at altitude, on consecutive days.Up to 1,200 mInca Trail, Patagonia W Trek
5 · Demanding7 to 9 hours on steep, sometimes exposed ground with hut nights in between.Over 1,200 mDolomites Hut to Hut

Booking a group trip

Dates, ages and private departures.

More answers on the questions page.

How far in advance should I book a small group tour?

For most trips, three to six months is enough. Book six to nine months ahead for the Inca Trail (government permits for May to August sell out early in the year), Patagonia in December and January, and any trip in Japan’s cherry-blossom or autumn-leaf weeks.

Can I book a group itinerary as a private trip?

Yes. Any trip on this page can run as a private departure for two or more people on dates you choose, with your own leader. For a couple the price is usually 20 to 35% higher per person than the group trip; for six or more it is often the same or lower. See private departures.

What age are people on small group tours?

Most of our travellers are between 35 and 70, and groups usually span two or three decades. The minimum age is between 12 and 16 depending on the trip, and anyone under 18 travels with a parent or guardian.

What does "Guaranteed" mean on a departure date?

A Guaranteed date will run even if only one or two people have booked, so you can buy flights straight away. Other dates are confirmed once four people have booked, and always at least 60 days before departure.
