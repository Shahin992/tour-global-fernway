Tailor-made or small group? How to choose

MSBy Mariko Sato, Japan specialist, tailor-made team
2 November 2026 · 10 min read

In short

Choose a small group tour if a departure fits your dates and you’re happy to share the pace and the table: it is the better value, and the company is part of the trip. Choose tailor-made if you need your own dates, travel with children or have a very specific idea of the trip, and expect to pay roughly 40 to 60% more per day. For groups of four or more, a private departure of a group itinerary often gives you the best of both.

What each actually costs: a worked example

Prices are easier to judge with a real trip. Here is our 11-day Kyoto and Kumano Kodo itinerary, costed three ways for April 2027, per person and excluding international flights:

  • Small group, up to 12 travellers: $5,290, or about $481 a day. The leader, minibus and luggage transfers are shared across the group.
  • Tailor-made for a couple, the same route in similar inns with a private guide on the walking days: about $7,800, or $709 a day. Every cost that a group shares is now split between two.
  • Private departure for six friends, with the same leader and minibus: about $5,300, almost exactly the group price, because six people share the fixed costs.

The pattern holds almost everywhere. The fixed costs of a trip, such as the leader, the vehicle and the driver, are what make private travel expensive for small parties and good value for larger ones.

Costs that are easy to miss

When you compare quotes, check what sits outside the headline price. Group trips usually include most dinners, all entry fees and airport transfers on the first and last day; tailor-made quotes sometimes list guides only on "key days" and leave meals entirely to you. A single supplement can add $450 to $900 to a group trip, while on a private trip a solo traveller pays for the whole vehicle. Comparing like with like often narrows the gap more than you’d expect.

When a small group trip is the better choice

A group trip is usually right if you can say yes to most of these:

  • One of the departure dates works for you.
  • You are travelling alone or as a couple and would enjoy meeting people.
  • It is your first visit to the region and you want an expert with you every day.
  • You would rather not spend weeks comparing hotels and train times.
  • You like having evenings planned, with the option to slip away.

Solo travellers in particular tend to be happiest on group trips. Travelling alone on a private itinerary is perfectly possible, but it is the most expensive way to travel and, by day eight, often a little lonely.

When tailor-made is worth the extra

Tailor-made earns its higher price when the group calendar or format simply doesn’t fit you:

  • Families with children, who need shorter days, family rooms and flexible plans.
  • Honeymoons and milestone trips, where the rooms and the moments matter more than the budget.
  • Special interests such as photography, birding, gardens or food, which need specialist guides and time.
  • Mobility or health needs, where every day can be planned around what works for you.
  • Combining regions, such as Japan’s Kumano Kodo with the art islands of the Inland Sea, which no group trip does.

The middle way: private departures

If you like a group itinerary but not the strangers, ask for a private departure. You get the same tested route, leader, inns and logistics on your own dates, for your own party. Because nothing has to be designed from scratch, it is quicker to arrange than a tailor-made trip, and from about six travellers it costs much the same per person as joining the group.

Private departures work especially well for multi-generation families, groups of friends celebrating something, and walking clubs. Read more on our private departures page.

Questions to ask any operator

  1. Has the person designing my trip actually been to these places, and recently?
  2. Which days have a guide, and which are self-guided?
  3. What exactly is included in the price, and what is a "guide price" based on?
  4. Who do I call at 11pm if a train is cancelled?
  5. How are my payments protected until I travel?
The best trip is the one that fits how you actually like to travel, not the one that looks best in the brochure.

Questions about choosing

Is tailor-made travel more expensive than a group tour?

Usually, yes. For a couple, a tailor-made version of the same route typically costs 40 to 60% more per day, because guides, drivers and vehicles are no longer shared. For parties of six or more, the difference largely disappears.

Can a tailor-made trip include days on a group tour?

Sometimes. We can add independent days before or after a group trip, or join you to a short group excursion such as a guided trek within a longer private journey. Your specialist will suggest what works.

Is a private departure the same as a tailor-made trip?

Not quite. A private departure runs an existing group itinerary just for your party, on your dates. A tailor-made trip is designed from scratch around you. Private departures are quicker to arrange and usually cheaper for groups.
