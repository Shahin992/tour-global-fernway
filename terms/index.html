Booking terms & conditions

The agreement between you and Fernway when you book a small-group trip, private departure or tailor-made journey. Please read it before paying a deposit.

1. Your contract

Your contract is with Fernway Journeys. It starts when we receive your deposit and send you a booking confirmation. The person making the booking accepts these terms on behalf of everyone named on it and must be at least 18.

2. Deposit and balance

A deposit of $500 per person is due at booking. The balance is due 60 days before departure; if you book within 60 days, the full price is due at booking. If the balance is not paid on time, we may treat the booking as cancelled by you and apply the charges in our refund policy.

3. Prices

Prices are per person in US dollars, based on sharing a room. Once your booking is confirmed, we will not add surcharges for fuel, currency or taxes. If the published price of your departure falls before you pay the balance, we will reduce your price to match.

4. Changes by you

You may transfer to another date or trip free of charge up to 60 days before departure, once per booking. After that, a change is treated as a cancellation. You may transfer your place to another person at any time, provided they meet the trip’s requirements and any permits can be reissued; an administration fee of $100 applies.

5. Cancellation by you

You may cancel at any time by writing to us. Cancellation charges depend on how much notice you give and are set out in full in our refund and cancellation policy. We strongly recommend insurance that covers cancellation.

6. Changes and cancellation by us

Itineraries are planned carefully, but local conditions sometimes require changes. We will tell you about any significant change as soon as possible. If we make a significant change before departure, you may accept it, transfer to another trip, or cancel for a full refund. We will not cancel your trip within 60 days of departure except for reasons beyond our control or minimum numbers as described below.

7. Minimum numbers and guaranteed departures

Departures marked Guaranteed run regardless of numbers. Other departures need at least four travellers and are confirmed no later than 60 days before departure. If a departure does not run, you may move to another date or trip, or receive a full refund of everything you have paid us.

8. Your responsibilities

  • Choose a trip suited to your fitness and health, and tell us about anything that might affect your participation.
  • Hold a valid passport, any visas and vaccinations required for your nationality.
  • Take out travel insurance covering medical treatment, emergency evacuation and cancellation.
  • Respect local laws, customs and the leader’s decisions on safety.

9. Your leader’s authority

Your leader may change the day’s plan for safety reasons, and may ask a traveller to leave the trip if their behaviour puts others at risk or seriously disrupts the group. In that case no refund is due.

10. Our responsibility to you

We are responsible for making sure the services in your itinerary are provided with reasonable skill and care, whether by us or by our suppliers. We are not responsible where a problem is caused by you, by a third party unconnected with your trip, or by unusual and unforeseeable circumstances beyond our control.

11. Complaints

If something goes wrong, tell your leader straight away so they can try to put it right. If it cannot be resolved on the spot, write to us within 28 days of returning and we will reply in full within 28 days.

12. Protection of your money

All payments for future trips are held in a protected client account, separate from our operating funds, and are released to us only after your trip has run.
