Booking & payments

How do I book, and how much is the deposit?

Choose a date on the trip page and send an enquiry, or call a planner. We hold your place for 48 hours while you read the booking terms, then a $500 per person deposit confirms it. You receive a booking confirmation and pre-departure guide by email.

When is the balance due?

The balance is due 60 days before departure. If you book within 60 days of departure, the full price is payable at booking. We accept bank transfer and major cards, with no card surcharges.

Is my money protected if the tour company fails?

Yes. All payments for future trips are held in a protected client account, separate from our operating funds, and are only released after your trip has run. If we could not operate a trip you had paid for, that money would be refunded to you.

What happens if a departure doesn’t reach the minimum number of travellers?

Departures marked Guaranteed run regardless of numbers. Other dates need four travellers and are confirmed at least 60 days before departure. If a date does not run, you can move to another date or trip, or receive a full refund of everything you have paid to us.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

You can change to another date or trip free of charge up to 60 days before departure. Cancellation charges depend on how close to departure you cancel, starting with the loss of your deposit; see our refund and cancellation policy for the full table.

Is the price fixed once I book?

Yes. We do not add fuel or currency surcharges after you book. If the price of your departure drops before you pay the balance, we will reduce yours to match.

On the trip

How fit do I need to be for a small group tour?

It depends on the trip’s activity level, from 1 (Leisurely) to 5 (Demanding). Level 2 means up to four hours of easy walking a day; level 4 means six to eight hours on mountain paths with big climbs. Every trip page describes the hardest day, and our activity level guide explains each level in detail.

What’s included in the price? Are flights included?

Prices include accommodation, your leader, all ground transport during the trip, entry fees and the meals listed in the itinerary. International flights, travel insurance, drinks and tips are not included. Each trip page lists exactly what is and isn’t covered.

Are small group tours good for solo travellers? Is there a single supplement?

Yes, about 40% of our travellers come alone. We pair you with a roommate of the same gender at no extra cost. If you’d prefer your own room, the single supplement is shown on each trip and is usually $450 to $900; some departures have a limited number of rooms with no supplement.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will brief every hotel and cook in advance. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and halal diets are straightforward on all our trips; severe allergies are possible on most, and we will tell you honestly where they are difficult.

Do I need travel insurance?

Yes. Insurance covering medical treatment, emergency evacuation and cancellation is a condition of travelling with us. For trekking trips above 4,000 m, check that your policy covers that altitude.

How much should I tip?

Tipping is never compulsory. Each trip page suggests an amount for the local team, typically $50 to $80 for the whole trip, which your leader collects at the end and shares fairly between drivers, porters and cooks.

Small groups, explained

What is a small group tour, and how many people are in the group?

A small group tour is a guided trip with a fixed itinerary and dates, shared with a handful of other travellers and led by a professional leader. Fernway groups are capped at 12 travellers (8 on safari) and averaged 9 in 2026, which means one minibus, smaller family-run inns and a group that can be invited into homes.

Are small group tours worth the money?

For most people, yes. A small group costs more per day than a large coach tour, but far less than a private trip with the same leader, transport and access. You also skip the planning, the logistics and the hours spent comparing hotels. Our guide to small group tours sets out the costs and the honest downsides.

Who travels on Fernway trips?

Curious, reasonably active people from more than forty countries. About 40% travel solo, a third are couples, and the rest are friends travelling together. English is the common language of every group.
