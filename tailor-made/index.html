Tailor-made · Private journeys

Possibilities, not packages.

Tell us where, when and how you like to travel. A specialist who has lived there will design a private trip around you, using the same leaders, inns and trails our small groups love.

How it works

Three steps, and we do the hard part.

Planning takes two to four weeks from first call to confirmed booking. There is no fee for the design, and no obligation until you pay a deposit.

  1. We listen

    A 30-minute video call with the specialist for your region: dates, budget, how active you want to be, who is travelling and what a perfect day looks like. We will also tell you honestly if a group trip would suit you better.

  2. We design your trip

    Within five working days you receive a day-by-day proposal with maps, hotel choices, a guide price and the reasons behind each choice. Change anything; most trips go through two or three drafts.

  3. We are with you while you travel

    A $500 deposit per person confirms everything. You get a printed travel book and app, a local partner meets you on arrival, and a 24-hour line connects you to someone who knows your plan.

What it costs

How much does a tailor-made trip cost per day?

Most of our tailor-made trips cost between $350 and $800 per person per day, excluding international flights. The biggest factors are your accommodation, private guiding days and the season.

Private departures

Any group trip, just for you.

Every itinerary on our trips page can run as a private departure for two or more people, on dates you choose, with your own leader. It is the simplest way to travel with family, friends or a walking club without designing a trip from scratch.

For a couple, a private departure usually costs 20 to 35% more per person than the group price. From six travellers it is often the same or less, and from eight you can take over a whole inn.

Tailor-made questions

Planning a trip together.

Something else? Ask a specialist.

Is a small group tour or a tailor-made trip better?

A small group tour is better value and comes with ready-made company and a leader; a tailor-made trip gives you your own dates, pace and rooms at a higher daily cost. If a group itinerary fits your dates, it is usually the better buy. We compare both in detail in Tailor-made or small group?

Do you book international flights?

We do not sell international flights, because our travellers fly from all over the world and usually find the best fares themselves. We will suggest arrival and departure times, and we book all internal flights, trains and ferries.

How far ahead should I plan a tailor-made trip?

Four to eight months ahead is ideal, and nine to twelve months for safaris in the migration season, Japan in cherry-blossom week or anywhere over Christmas. We can plan trips at shorter notice, with a little less choice of rooms.

Is there a fee for the itinerary design?

No. Design and revisions are free. You pay a $500 per person deposit only when you are ready to confirm, and the balance 60 days before you travel.
